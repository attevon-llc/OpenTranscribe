## What is OpenTranscribe?

OpenTranscribe is a powerful, self-hosted AI transcription platform featuring:

- 🎧 **High-accuracy transcription** with WhisperX (70x realtime on GPU)
- 👥 **Smart speaker detection** with cross-video recognition
- 🤖 **AI-powered insights** using local or cloud LLMs
- 🔍 **Hybrid search** with full-text and semantic capabilities
- 🔒 **Privacy-first** - all data stays on your infrastructure
